F*cking around and finding out has been attributed to brain rot content in social media for a while. Especially when it comes to someone getting their face smacked or a hilarious fall. The videos do a great job of showing what it feels like to fail when f*cking around. But what I haven't seen portrayed often is the act of f*cking around and finding out as an art of moving around life, which sometimes leads to valuable insights and lessons one can carry for the rest of one's life.

So what do I mean by f*cking around and finding out?

F*ck Around

f*ck around phrasal verb
To have the curiosity and courage to try things despite fear, judgment, and/or lack of experience.

Note: all within the good morals of a good citizen of the world. This doesn't mean you're going to open the plane's emergency exit just for the sake of finding out what happens and putting other people's lives at risk.

Basically, what this means to me is that you have a crazy scientist's, a philosopher's, and a dummy (what I mean by dummy is like the test dummy) hat on most of the time. And you like pushing buttons and moving levers because curiosity drives you to know what happens next. This can be applied in many areas of life, such as professional, personal, and spiritual. It depends solely on you and what sub-area you want to experiment with.

Finding out

Once you push the button or pull the lever, you… find out. Sometimes the result is instant and sometimes it takes years to see the repercussions of such actions, but you're getting yet another data point that's going to be useful in life. Some of these are positive and some of these could be negative. And you need to be the judge of whether it makes sense to push that button again or not. Or to go through the door the lever you pulled opened.

The point of this

Sometimes I think this street philosophy serves the purpose of making experience the lab/testing site of which we're part of. It opens the possibility for failures and discoveries, all with the aim of making progress and discovering new frontiers and capabilities of our own and the reality we're part of.

So keep on f*cking around and finding out.
